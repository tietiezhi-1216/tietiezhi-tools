E · Authored Form / 自定之形
File: e-authored-form.png

Use case: logo-brand.
PROJECT: Untitled, a macOS-only application that lets people create, install, share, and reshape their own agent-built software plugins. It deliberately has no single flagship feature. Its identity should communicate authorship, possibility, independence, and a system that is continually becoming something new.
BRAND NAME: Untitled. Treat the name as a statement that the user defines the software. Do not interpret it as a literal blank document, an unfinished loading state, or a missing label.
USER TASTE: exclusively black and white, flat and minimal at first glance, with surprising sophistication on closer inspection. The user has rejected generic geometric assemblies, detached little squares, open frames, rings, letter U monograms, underscores, and stock-looking abstract blobs. They want stronger art direction and a distinctive silhouette, not more decoration.
DESIGN STANDARD: create an original emblem that feels commissioned from a superb independent identity studio. It must have a specific visual idea, intentional optical corrections, satisfying tension between solid mass and negative space, and recognition at small sizes. An asymmetrical form can be optically balanced. Avoid making every radius identical or making the composition rigidly mathematical if that reduces character.
COLOR AND MEDIUM: only pure black ink and pure white negative space. Flat two-dimensional graphic artwork, extremely crisp edges, no paper grain or any texture. Natural edge antialiasing is acceptable. No gradients, gray shading, shadows, dimensional rendering, bevels, highlights, glass, metallic surfaces, outlines added around the artwork, or lighting effects.
ICON COMPOSITION: one finished 1024x1024 square macOS app icon study. Use a solid background filling the complete image. Place a single emblem in the central 62 percent of the canvas, with deliberate breathing room on every side. This is the artwork for an app icon, not a photograph of an icon and not a presentation board. No grid, margin guides, color swatches, numbers, captions, annotations, wordmark, or explanatory text.
LEGIBILITY: at 16–32 pixels, the overall silhouette should still read clearly. Internal openings must be broad enough to survive reduction. Avoid filigree, little disconnected specks, thin cuts, crowded crossings, or tiny details. The sophistication must come from the contour and counterforms, not complexity added to the surface.
EXCLUSIONS: no house, roof, doorway, nest, home metaphor, robot, brain, magic wand, gear, puzzle-piece jigsaw, terminal prompt, cursor, code bracket, power button, star, sparkle, camera shutter, literal page or sheet of paper, cube, hexagonal AI knot, infinity symbol, or familiar technology-company emblem. Do not include letters or readable typography. Avoid a symbol that accidentally looks like a smiley, animal face, or anatomical form.

CREATIVE IDEA: a single continuous, sculptural black stroke that seems to have been authored in one confident motion, then carefully refined into an unmistakable emblem. It should feel simultaneously drawn and constructed, expressing a thought becoming its own tool.
FORM DEVELOPMENT: begin with the dynamic tension of a calligraphic gesture, but remove all brush texture and expressive splatter. Translate it into a bold, precise silhouette with two broad changes of direction. One edge should travel through a generous convex arc; another should make a decisive, short angular interruption. Let the stroke subtly vary in width, becoming substantial where the form carries visual weight and more restrained where it turns.
NEGATIVE SPACE: one unconventional white counterform passes through the center and opens out to the exterior. Its contour should echo the outer form without tracing it exactly. Make this opening the secondary signature of the emblem. The opening must feel like something can continue to emerge through it; do not draw a literal tunnel or door.
GESTURE: slightly diagonal overall movement, with a quiet stable center of gravity. The top and bottom should have different rhythms. Avoid bilateral symmetry and a circular outer footprint. Do not form a ribbon loop, numeral, known letter, or swoosh.
FINISH: black emblem on pure white. Compact, confident, distinctive, optically balanced. Imagine the emblem used alone on a Dock icon, as a tiny menu-bar mark, or debossed without any brand name: it must have enough character to belong to Untitled.

CREATIVE IDEA: an original white emblem that feels like a small system capable of reorganizing itself. Its parts are inseparable from the whole; the image should suggest flexibility and purposeful connection without using puzzle pieces or ordinary modular squares.
FORM DEVELOPMENT: build one compact, connected silhouette with three substantial directional arms of deliberately unequal length. Give the contour a controlled alternation of long calm curves and short decisive flat cuts. The outer footprint should feel almost square without becoming a rounded square, clover, pinwheel, propeller, flower, or three-lobed blob.
NEGATIVE SPACE: two broad black counterforms flow through the white mass, one opening toward the upper edge, the other opening toward the lower-left edge. Their shapes should be related but not mirrored. Where they approach the center, preserve a bold white bridge, so the emblem stays a single coherent object. These two counterforms are the visual argument: independently shaped capabilities finding a common structure.
OPTICAL DETAIL: vary the curvature intentionally so every transition has purpose. Keep the center slightly off-axis to create intelligent asymmetry, and compensate through the length and weight of the outer arms. The mark should reward a second look while remaining simple in silhouette.
FINISH: white emblem on pure black. Strong, calm, editorial, a little unconventional. Distinct from generic SaaS icons and familiar AI-company symbols. No rotational repetition, no decorative notches, and no additional small marks.
