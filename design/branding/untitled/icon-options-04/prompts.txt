The visual may be complex and layered; do not simplify it into a generic symbol merely for small-size legibility.

White background. Create a dense, asymmetrical burst of jet-black fluid in turbulent motion, captured as a remarkable two-dimensional ink silhouette. Think of colliding currents, stretched membranes, unexpected pockets of white, folding edges, thin filaments that thicken into heavy pools. The shape should have a powerful off-center center of gravity and a few beautiful unpredictable tendrils, not a regular radial splash or a round inkblot. Its internal white voids should have different sizes, directions and rhythms. A balance of substantial black mass and airy ragged openings, with a raw, almost biological energy. The result should feel both chaotic and exquisitely composed, like experimental Japanese ink art translated into a modern app identity. Not a literal wave, flame, dragon, bird, skull or brush-written letter.
